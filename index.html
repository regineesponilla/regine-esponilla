About Me

The story behind the CV

I did not plan to end up in Ireland. I planned to keep building my career in the Philippines in financial operations, then sales, then digital marketing. But when the opportunity came to pursue a Master's degree at the Technological University of the Shannon in Limerick, I said yes — and gave it everything I had.

First Class Honours. GPA 72.77. The Student Award for Excellence. The Silver TUS Employability Plus Award. Two live consultancy projects for real clients, one in the UK, one right here in Limerick, where my work was taken seriously enough to be formally adopted by senior stakeholders.

What I have learned over eleven years across five industries is this: the best decisions, in marketing, in sales, in business, come from the same place. You have to understand what the data is telling you. And then you have to have the confidence to act on it.

"I am not someone who separates marketing from analysis, or sales from strategy. For me, they have always been the same conversation, just using different tools."
